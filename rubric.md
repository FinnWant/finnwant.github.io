Score each criterion 1-10. Anchors: 5 = functional but plain or inconsistent;
7 = clean and competent, a few rough edges; 9 = polished, distinctive, would hold up
next to a top-tier professional site; 10 = exceptional, nothing to improve.

- hierarchy: The most important content is obvious at a glance; headings, emphasis and
  calls to action lead the eye in a clear order.
- typography: Consistent type scale, comfortable line length (45-75 chars) and line
  height, readable sizes, no more than two typefaces used purposefully.
- color: Cohesive palette, sufficient text contrast (WCAG AA), color used to signal
  meaning rather than decoration.
- layout: Elements align to a consistent grid; spacing follows a regular rhythm;
  whitespace separates groups clearly; nothing feels cramped or floating.
- responsiveness: Works at phone width: no horizontal scroll, readable text, tap
  targets at least ~44px, layout adapts rather than just shrinking.
- consistency: Buttons, links, cards and other components look and behave the same
  everywhere; the pages feel like one site.
- polish: Details are finished: hover/focus states, image cropping, icon sizing,
  no orphaned elements or leftover default browser styles.
